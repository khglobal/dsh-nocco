
shadcn with Tailwind
- Components live in `components/ui/` and are auto-imported by name: Avatar, Badge, Button, Card, Dialog, DropdownMenu, Input, Label, NavigationMenu, Select, Separator, Sheet, Tabs, Textarea, with their parts. Leave that folder as it is.
- Style with Tailwind classes. `cn()` from `~/lib/utils` merges class names. Icons come from `lucide-vue-next`.
- `layouts/default.vue` exists and is yours to change.
